Add Solid-native `Combobox`, `Tooltip`, `Drawer` and `DockLayout` controls to the Lua runtime, as `gpuix.solid.combobox`, `gpuix.solid.tooltip`, `gpuix.solid.drawer` and `gpuix.solid.dock_layout`, or from `require("gpuix.solid.controls")`. The Solid workspace example shows all eight Solid controls.
